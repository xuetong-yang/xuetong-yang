## Xuetong Yang

M.Sc. student in Computational Linguistics at the Institute for Natural Language Processing (IMS), University of Stuttgart.
My focus is applied natural language processing, in particular text classification, text mining, and the conversion of unstructured language data into structured formats for analysis.
Prior to my studies, I spent eight years in content marketing and digital operations.

### Selected projects

| Project | Methods | Results |
|---|---|---|
| [Explainable Emotion Detection](https://github.com/xuetong-yang/explainable-emotion-detection) | Seven-class emotion classification on ISEAR. Comparison of a perceptron baseline implemented from scratch, TF-IDF logistic regression, and fine-tuned RoBERTa. Error analysis with SHAP. | RoBERTa achieves a macro-F1 of 0.71. Error analysis identifies shame and guilt as the most frequently confused classes. |
| [Customer Review Analysis](https://github.com/xuetong-yang/customer-review-analysis) | Aspect-based sentiment annotation of Yelp reviews. XML Schema validation and XPointer anchoring. Relational modelling in PostgreSQL with window functions and full-text search. Knowledge graph with federated SPARQL queries to Wikidata. Three-person team project. | Service quality is the main differentiator between brands: 82% of McDonald's reviews rate service negatively, while 32% of Burger King reviews rate it positively. |
| [Audio Deepfake Detection](https://github.com/xuetong-yang/audio-deepfake-detection) | Binary spoofing detection on LFCC features with a 1D CNN. Weighted EER of 2.50%, ranked 7th of 59 submissions. |
